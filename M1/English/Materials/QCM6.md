# Réponses commentées au QCM de Remise à Niveau n°6

## Multiple Choice

1. The journalists were given _____ information about the crisis.

a. very few
b. fewer
c. far many
d. very little

- 答案：d) very little
- 说明：选项 a) very few 错误，因为`few` 只能修饰可数名词复数，information 为不可数名词。；选项 b) fewer 错误，因为`fewer` 也只能修饰可数名词复数，不能搭配 information。；选项 c) far many 错误，因为`far many` 并非标准搭配。；选项 d) very little 正确，因为`little` 修饰不可数名词，表示数量很少。

2. You _____ tried to meet him while you were in New York!

a. should to have
b. had to
c. ought to have
d. must

- 答案：c) ought to have
- 说明：选项 a) should to have 错误，因为`should` 后直接接动词原形，不能加 `to`。；选项 b) had to 错误，因为`had to` 表示过去必须做的事，不含责备语气。；选项 c) ought to have 正确，因为`ought to have done` 表示过去该做却没做。；选项 d) must 错误，因为单独的 `must` 只能表示现在或将来的必要性。

3. If the firemen hadn't arrived quickly, his house _____.

a. might have been destroyed
b. could have been destroying
c. was burning
d. should have destroyed

- 答案：a) might have been destroyed
- 说明：选项 a) might have been destroyed 正确，因为第三条件句结果从句用完成式虚拟语气。；选项 b) could have been destroying 错误，因为被动结构需用过去分词 destroyed。；选项 c) was burning 错误，因为过去进行时不能表达未发生的假设结果。；选项 d) should have destroyed 错误，因为house 不能作 destroy 的施事，也不符虚拟语气。

4. Driving has always been more _____ than flying.

a. economic
b. economical
c. costly
d. expensive

- 答案：b) **economical**
- 说明：选项 a) economic 错误，因为`economic` 表示与经济相关，不用于比较费用。；选项 b) economical 正确，因为`economical` 表示省钱的。；选项 c) costly 错误，因为costly 与 more 比较会产生相反含义。；选项 d) expensive 错误，因为more expensive 表示更贵，与语境相反。

5. His car _____ was seriously damaged last year, has been nicely repaired.

a. , that
b. , which
c. , what
d. that

- 答案：b) , which
- 说明：选项 a) , that 错误，因为非限定性定语从句不用 that，且前不能有逗号。；选项 b) , which 正确，因为非限定性定语从句应使用 , which。；选项 c) , what 错误，因为what 不能引导非限定性定语从句。；选项 d) that 错误，因为缺少逗号，且 that 不用于此类从句。

6. Their policy on that issue is quite different _____ ours.

a. of
b. than
c. from
d. as

- 答案：c) from
- 说明：选项 a) of 错误，因为different of 不是固定搭配。；选项 b) than 错误，因为常用搭配是 different from。；选项 c) from 正确，因为固定搭配 **different from**；选项 d) as 错误，因为different as 不搭配。

7. Much _____ I would love to come with you, I still have too much to do.

a. like
b. that
c. how
d. as

- 答案：d) as
- 说明：选项 a) like 错误，因为much like 表示‘很像’，不是让步结构。；选项 b) that 错误，因为much that 不成搭配。；选项 c) how 错误，因为much how 不合语法。；选项 d) as 正确，因为**Much as...** 表示‘尽管非常...’。

8. _____ is a journalist's inalienable right.

a. Freedom of the press
b. Freedom of press
c. The freedom of press
d. The freedom of the press

- 答案：a) Freedom of the press
- 说明：选项 a) **Freedom of the press** 正确，因为固定表达，前面不加冠词。；选项 b) Freedom of press 错误，因为缺少 the。；选项 c) The freedom of press 错误，因为press 前应加 the 或省略冠词。；选项 d) The freedom of the press 错误，因为该句泛指权利，通常不用定冠词引导。

9. My boss never loses precious time, he always gets things _____ immediately.

a. to be
b. doing done
c. done
d. to be doing

- 答案：c) done
- 说明：选项 a) to be 错误，因为使役动词 get 后接过去分词或形容词。；选项 b) doing done 错误，因为结构混乱，语法不通。；选项 c) done 正确，因为 **get things done** 表示让事情完成。；选项 d) to be doing 错误，因为to be doing 与 get 不搭配。

10. It isn't worth _____ her, she'll never accept.

a. inviting
b. to invite
c. to be inviting
d. invite

- 答案：a) inviting
- 说明：选项 a) inviting 正确，因为**worth 后接动名词**。；选项 b) to invite 错误，因为worth 后不能接不定式。；选项 c) to be inviting 错误，因为结构不合语法。；选项 d) invite 错误，因为不能直接使用动词原形。

11. Whenever you've doubts about a calculation, have it _____ by John.

a. check
b. checking
c. checked
d. to check

- 答案：c) checked
- 说明：选项 a) check 错误，因为**have + 受词 + 过去分词** 表示使役，被动意义。；选项 b) checking 错误，因为现在分词表示正在进行，与语境不符。；选项 c) checked 正确，因为have something checked 表示让人检查。；选项 d) to check 错误，因为不定式不能表达完成的检查动作。

12. The healing power of that product _____ for ages.

a. was known
b. has been knowing
c. has been known
d. is known

- 答案：c) has been known
- 说明：选项 a) was known 错误，因为过去式无法体现持续到现在。；选项 b) has been knowing 错误，因为know 是状态动词，不能用进行时。；选项 c) has been known 正确，因为**现在完成时被动表示长期以来**众所周知。；选项 d) is known 错误，因为一般现在时不足以强调持续时间。

13. Countries like Belgium and _____ were not negligible for European construction.

a. Switzerland
b. the Netherlands
c. Netherlands
d. the Italy

- 答案：b) the Netherlands
- 说明：选项 a) Switzerland 错误，因为缺少并列国家，句意不完整。；选项 b) **the Netherlands** 正确，因为国名 the Netherlands 需带定冠词，和 Belgium 并列。；选项 c) Netherlands 错误，因为缺少定冠词 the。；选项 d) the Italy 错误，因为Italy 前一般不加 the。

14. I feel confident about our future growth. Our company has made _____.

a. a great benefit
b. important profit
c. a substantial profit
d. an interesting benefit

- 答案：c) a substantial profit
- 说明：选项 a) a great benefit 错误，因为benefit 不与 make 搭配表示盈利。；选项 b) important profit 错误，因为缺少冠词，且 important 不常修饰 profit。；选项 c) a substantial profit 正确，因为 **make a substantial profit** 为固定表达。；选项 d) an interesting benefit 错误，因为语义不贴合，且 benefit 仍不搭配 make。

15. My brother's band _____ six times in Paris since last year.

a. performed
b. had performed
c. has performed
d. has been performing

- 答案：c) has performed
- 说明：选项 a) performed 错误，因为一般过去时只强调过去某次事件。；选项 b) had performed 错误，因为过去完成时需另一个过去参照点。；选项 c) has performed 正确，因为since last year 表示至今重复发生，用现在完成时。；选项 d) has been performing 错误，因为完成进行时强调动作持续，不表示次数。

16. It is a small airline. Its fleet only has twenty _____.

a. airfare
b. airfares
c. aircraft
d. aircrafts

- 答案：c) aircraft
- 说明：选项 a) airfare 错误，因为airfare 指机票价格。；选项 b) airfares 错误，因为复数仍指票价。；选项 c) aircraft 正确，因为aircraft 单复同形表示飞机。；选项 d) aircrafts 错误，因为aircraft 不加 s。

17. I'll never forgive you if you don't _____ next time you are in Paris.

a. let me down
b. call me in
c. look me up
d. see me out

- 答案：c) look me up
- 说明：选项 a) let me down 错误，因为**let someone down**表示让人失望。；选项 b) call me in 错误，因为call in 需接 on somebody 才表示拜访。；选项 c) look me up 正确，因为 **look someone up** 表示顺道拜访。；选项 d) see me out 错误，因为**see someone out** 表示送客。

18. Most politicians think that _____ currency would be good for their country.

a. a unique
b. an only
c. a single
d. an alone

- 答案：c) a single
- 说明：选项 a) a unique 错误，因为unique 不直接修饰名词，需要 a unique + 名词。；选项 b) an only 错误，因为only 前不接 an，且缺名词。；选项 c) a single 正确，因为 **a single currency** 表示统一货币。；选项 d) an alone 错误，因为alone 不能作定语。

19. Sarah was out when I phoned. Her mother advised me to _____ tonight.

a. call again
b. call back
c. recall
d. retelephone

- 答案：b) call back
- 说明：选项 a) call again 错误，因为缺少‘回电’含义。；选项 b) **call back** 正确，因为call back 表示回电话。；选项 c) recall 错误，因为recall 意为回忆或召回。；选项 d) retelephone 错误，因为几乎不用且生硬。

20. The rain has _____, The sun is finally shining.

a. let down
b. let up
c. gone down
d. gone up

- 答案：b) let up
- 说明：选项 a) let down 错误，因为 **let down** 表示让人失望。；选项 b) let up 正确，因为 **let up** 表示减弱或停止。；选项 c) gone down 错误，因为 **go down** 表示下降，与雨停不符。；选项 d) gone up 错误，因为 **go up** 表示上升。

21. Things have been _____ in Jim's career for six months.

a. looking up
b. looking down
c. taking on
d. holding down

- 答案：a) looking up
- 说明：选项 a) looking up 正确，因为 **look up** 表示情况好转。；选项 b) looking down 错误，因为**look down** 多指俯视或轻视。；选项 c) taking on 错误，因为**take on** 指承担、雇用。；选项 d) holding down 错误，因为**hold down** 表示压制或勉强保住工作。

22. Mrs Simpson _____ for 12 years when it was disclosed she'd bequeathed a fortune to her gardener.

a. had been working
b. has been working
c. was working
d. worked

- 答案：a) had been working
- 说明：选项 a) had been working 正确，因为**过去完成进行时强调在披露前已持续 12 年**。；选项 b) has been working 错误，因为现在完成进行时与过去时间参照不符。；选项 c) was working 错误，因为过去进行时不强调持续长度。；选项 d) worked 错误，因为一般过去时缺少持续含义。

23. He has become the most _____ person in his field.

a. seeked-for
b. sought
c. sought-for
d. sought-after

- 答案：d) sought-after
- 说明：选项 a) seeked-for 错误，因为seek 的过去分词是 sought。；选项 b) sought 错误，因为Sought 缺少 after 才表示受欢迎。；选项 c) sought-for 错误，因为较少使用且生硬。；选项 d) **sought-after** 正确，因为sought-after 表示**抢手的**。

24. They _____ leave the office unless they _____

a. would not / get what they want
b. will not / get what they want
c. will not / will get what they want
d. will not / got what they want

- 答案：b) will not / get what they want **主将从现**
- 说明：选项 a) would not / get what they want 错误，因为主句用 would not 与真实条件不符。；选项 b) will not / get what they want 正确，因为主句将来时，从句一般现在表条件。；选项 c) will not / will get what they want 错误，因为条件从句不使用 will。；选项 d) will not / got what they want 错误，因为条件从句要用一般现在时，不能用过去式 got。

25. The authorities are now trying to negotiate with the terrorists; everyone inside the building is holding _____ breath.

a. its
b. their
c. his
d. one's

- 答案：b) their
- 说明：选项 a) its 错误，因为指人时不用 its。；选项 b) their 正确，因为单数 they/their 指泛指的人。；选项 c) his 错误，因为使用男性代词不够包容。；选项 d) one's 错误，因为one's 需与主语 one 对应。

26. "The weather is fine now. Must I still put on my sweater?" "No, you _____."

a. mustn't
b. shouldn't
c. needn't
d. haven't

- 答案：c) needn't
- 说明：选项 a) mustn't 错误，因为mustn't 表示禁止。；选项 b) shouldn't 错误，因为shouldn't 表示不应该，语气过强。；选项 c) needn't 正确，因为needn't 表示没有必要。；选项 d) haven't 错误，因为haven't 后需宾语且不表示无需。

27. All the students were asked to _____ the lecture on US foreign policies.

a. assist
b. attend to
c. attend
d. take part at

- 答案：c) attend
- 说明：选项 a) assist 错误，因为assist 表示帮助。；选项 b) attend to 错误，因为**attend to** 表示处理。；选项 c) attend 正确，因为 **attend the lecture** 表示出席。；选项 d) take part at 错误，因为固定搭配应为 **take part in**。

28. "Let's go now, _____?"

a. let we
b. do we
c. will we
d. shall we

- 答案：d) shall we
- 说明：选项 a) let we 错误，因为语序和结构错误。；选项 b) do we 错误，因为祈使句的附加问句不用 do we。；选项 c) will we 错误，因为祈使句通常用 shall we。；选项 d) shall we 正确，因为祈使句附加问句用 shall we。

29. Reading the press regularly is a good _____ to improve one's English.

a. mean
b. means
c. mine
d. mines

- 答案：b) means
- 说明：选项 a) mean 错误，因为单数形式应为 a means。；选项 b) means 正确，因为a means 表示方法，名词单复同形。；选项 c) mine 错误，因为mine 指矿或‘我的’，不合语境。；选项 d) mines 错误，因为mines 指矿井。

30. The Americans usually have ______ cereals for ______ breakfast.

a. ∅ / the
b. the / a
c. ∅ / ∅
d. the / ∅

- 答案：c) ∅ / ∅
- 说明：选项 a) ∅ / the 错误，因为泛指早餐时 breakfast 前不加 the。；选项 b) the / a 错误，因为cereals 泛指时不用 the，breakfast 也不加 a。；选项 c) ∅ / ∅ 正确，因为谈论一般饮食习惯时两个名词都不加冠词。；选项 d) the / ∅ 错误，因为cereals 不需 the。

31. Phil is the _____ of the two Simpson kids.

a. elder
b. more old
c. eldest
d. most old

- 答案：a) elder
- 说明：选项 a) elder 正确，因为the elder 用于两人比较。；选项 b) more old 错误，因为应为 older。；选项 c) eldest 错误，因为eldest 通常用于三人及以上。；选项 d) most old 错误，因为比较结构错误。

32. Would you be _____ kind ______ hold this for me?

a. so / to
b. as / and to
c. so / as to
d. so / so as

- 答案：c) so / as to
- 说明：选项 a) so / to 错误，因为缺少 as 连接。；选项 b) as / and to 错误，因为连接词错误。；选项 c) so / as to 正确，因为**be so kind as to do** 为固定表达。；选项 d) so / so as 错误，因为**so as 后需动词不定式**。

33. The victim's body _____ was found in the lake, behind her house.

a. eventually
b. lastly
c. at the least
d. in the least

- 答案：a) eventually
- 说明：选项 a) eventually 正确，因为表示‘最终’。；选项 b) lastly 错误，因为 lastly 多用于列举项目。；选项 c) at the least 错误，因为意为‘至少’，不合语境。；选项 d) in the least 错误，因为意为‘丝毫’，语义不贴合。

34. Question: "Which of the two brothers are you alluding to?" Answer: "_____".

a. The older
b. The elder
c. The eldest
d. The old

- 答案：b) The elder
- 说明：选项 a) The older 错误，因为虽可理解，但传统上用 the elder。；选项 b) The elder 正确，因为专指两人中年长者。；选项 c) The eldest 错误，因为用于三人及以上。；选项 d) The old 错误，因为表达不自然。

35. I am afraid you'll have to deal again, I have one card_____.

a. too much
b. too more
c. enough
d. too many

- 答案：d) too many
- 说明：选项 a) too much 错误，因为much 修饰不可数名词。；选项 b) too more 错误，因为比较结构错误。；选项 c) enough 错误，因为表示足够，不是多余。；选项 d) too many 正确，因为one card too many 表示多了一张。

36. They don't have a lot of money but they are _____ the worse _____ it.

a. none / for
b. none / about
c. any / for
d. no / for

- 答案：a) none / for
- 说明：选项 a) none / for 正确，因为**none the worse for** 表示并不因此更糟。；选项 b) none / about 错误，因为搭配不存在。；选项 c) any / for 错误，因为any the worse for 不常用。；选项 d) no / for 错误，因为缺少 the worse 做比较级。

37. Every night, the TV anchor says: "Good evening! _____ the six o'clock news."

a. Here are
b. These are
c. This is
d. That is

- 答案：c) This is
- 说明：选项 a) Here are 错误，因为news 被视作不可数单数，不能用复数动词。；选项 b) These are 错误，因为同上。；选项 c) **This is** 正确，因为与单数 news 保持一致。；选项 d) That is 错误，因为that 表示远指，与开场白不符。

38. I like driving my car but I hate travelling as a passenger in _____.

a. the one of else
b. the one of anyone else
c. someone else's
d. anyone elses's

- 答案：c) someone else's
- 说明：选项 a) the one of else 错误，因为语法不通。；选项 b) the one of anyone else 错误，因为缺少所有格，结构生硬。；选项 c) someone else's 正确，因为表示别人的车。；选项 d) anyone elses's 错误，因为所有格拼写错误。

39. I'm fed up with this bank of yours! Let me talk to _____!

a. the responsible
b. the responsible person
c. the in-charge person
d. the person in charge

- 答案：d) the person in charge
- 说明：选项 a) the responsible 错误，因为responsible 不能单独作名词。；选项 b) the responsible person 错误，因为可理解但不地道。；选项 c) the in-charge person 错误，因为in charge 应放名词后。；选项 d) the person in charge 正确，因为标准说法。

40. Is this seat _____ ?

a. busy
b. any busy
c. free
d. any free

- 答案：c) free
- 说明：选项 a) busy 错误，因为busy 用于形容人或活动。；选项 b) any busy 错误，因为结构不通。；选项 c) free 正确，因为询问座位是否空闲用 free。；选项 d) any free 错误，因为应说 any free seats。
