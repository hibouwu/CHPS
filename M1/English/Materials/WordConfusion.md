## Word Confusion

1. You _ BEAT/WON me the last time we played tennis.

- 答案：BEAT
- 说明：选项 BEAT 正确，因为beat someone 表示在比赛中打败某人。；选项 WON 错误，因为win 后通常接比赛或奖项，不能直接接人。

2. Who _ WON/BEAT the gold medal in the 100 meter dash?

- 答案：WON
- 说明：选项 WON 正确，因为win a medal 表示赢得奖牌。；选项 BEAT 错误，因为beat 后应接对手而非奖项。

3. Could you please _BORROW/LEND me your laptop?

- 答案：LEND
- 说明：选项 BORROW 错误，因为borrow 表示向别人借入，应说 borrow something from somebody。；选项 LEND 正确，因为lend somebody something 表示借出给某人。

4. I need to _ BORROW/LEND someone's badge so I can get in the building.

- 答案：BORROW
- 说明：选项 BORROW 正确，因为从别人那里借东西要用 borrow。；选项 LEND 错误，因为lend 表示借出，与语境不符。

5. I'm having trouble LISTENING/HEARING you. Could you please speak a little louder?

- 答案：HEARING
- 说明：选项 LISTENING 错误，因为listen 强调主动聆听，与‘听不见’不符。；选项 HEARING 正确，因为hear 指能否听到声音。

6. I _ LISTENING/HEARING to podcasts in English. It's a great way to learn.

- 答案：LISTENING
- 说明：选项 LISTENING 正确，因为enjoy listening to 表示喜爱收听。；选项 HEARING 错误，因为hear 不表示主动收听习惯。

7. Did you hear what happened to Miguel? Somebody ROBBED/STOLE him while he was walking home from work.

- 答案：ROBBED
- 说明：选项 ROBBED 正确，因为rob someone 表示抢劫某人。；选项 STOLE 错误，因为steal 的宾语通常是被偷的物品。

8. I can't believe you _ ROBBED/STOLE _ my idea and passed it off as your own.

- 答案：STOLE
- 说明：选项 ROBBED 错误，因为rob 不以想法作宾语。；选项 STOLE 正确，因为steal someone's idea 表示偷主意。

9. I had a lot of FUNNY/FUN at the company picnic.

- 答案：FUN
- 说明：选项 FUNNY 错误，因为funny 是形容词。；选项 FUN 正确，因为fun 作名词表示乐趣。

10. His jokes are terrible. Nobody thinks they're FUNNY/FUN

- 答案：FUNNY
- 说明：选项 FUNNY 正确，因为funny 形容好笑。；选项 FUN 错误，因为fun 很少修饰 jokes。

11. I like my computer, but _ ITS/IT'S a little slow.

- 答案：IT'S
- 说明：选项 ITS 错误，因为缺少动词。；选项 IT'S 正确，因为it's = it is。

12. My computer is great, but ITS/IT'S operating system isn't very good.

- 答案：ITS
- 说明：选项 ITS 正确，因为its 表示它的。；选项 IT'S 错误，因为it is operating system 不合语法。

13. I think something is _LOSE/LOOSE_ inside of my smart phone. When I shake it, it makes a rattling noise.

- 答案：LOOSE
- 说明：选项 LOSE 错误，因为lose 是动词。；选项 LOOSE 正确，因为loose 形容松动。

14. If we don't pay competitive wages, we'll LOSE/LOOSE some of our employees to our competitors.

- 答案：LOSE
- 说明：选项 LOSE 正确，因为lose 表示失去员工。；选项 LOOSE 错误，因为loose 是形容词。

15. She WISHES/HOPES to get a promotion next year.

- 答案：HOPES
- 说明：选项 WISHES 错误，因为wish to do 太正式且不一定现实。；选项 HOPES 正确，因为hope to do 表示现实期望。

16. I really WISHES/HOPES I had more time to finish these reports.

- 答案：WISH
- 说明：选项 WISH 正确，因为wish + 过去式 表达与现实相反的愿望。；选项 HOPES 错误，因为hope 不和虚拟语气搭配且主语 I 不用第三人称形式。

17. We started as a small company, but we've grown up grown to a large corporation.

- 答案：GROWN
- 说明：选项 GROWN 正确，因为grow to 表示发展壮大。；选项 GROWN UP 错误，因为grow up 多指人长大。

18. You act so immature. You really need to ____GROW /GROW UP

- 答案：GROW UP
- 说明：选项 GROW 错误，因为grow 不说明成熟。；选项 GROW UP 正确，因为grow up 表示成熟起来。

19. We've gone on many business TRIPS /TRAVELS together.

- 答案：TRIPS
- 说明：选项 TRIPS 正确，因为trip 可数，表示一次次行程。；选项 TRAVELS 错误，因为travels 通常指旅行经历的总称。

20. When are you going to TRIP/TRAVEL to Peru?

- 答案：TRAVEL
- 说明：选项 TRIP 错误，因为trip 是名词。；选项 TRAVEL 正确，因为travel 作动词表示旅行。

21. I don't believe his _STORY/ HISTORY. I think he made it up.

- 答案：STORY
- 说明：选项 STORY 正确，因为story 指个人故事，可编造。；选项 HISTORY 错误，因为history 指历史或经历。

22. I can provide you with details about my employment _STORY/HISTORY if you'd like.

- 答案：HISTORY
- 说明：选项 STORY 错误，因为employment story 不是固定说法。；选项 HISTORY 正确，因为employment history 指工作履历。

23. It's a popular vacation spot and there are a lot of ____ FOREIGNERS / STRANGERS here during the summer.

- 答案：FOREIGNERS
- 说明：选项 FOREIGNERS 正确，因为foreigners 指外国人。；选项 STRANGERS 错误，因为strangers 仅指陌生人。

24. No, I've never seen him in my life. He's a complete ____FOREIGNER / STRANGER to me.

- 答案：STRANGER
- 说明：选项 FOREIGNER 错误，因为foreigners 强调国籍不同。；选项 STRANGER 正确，因为stranger 表示完全不认识的人。

25. We have our weekly MEETINGS/ REUNIONS on Tuesday mornings.

- 答案：MEETINGS
- 说明：选项 MEETINGS 正确，因为例行会议用 meeting。；选项 REUNIONS 错误，因为reunion 指久别重逢。

26. They had an emotional MEETING/REUNION at the airport when Dave arrived after having traveled for months.

- 答案：REUNION
- 说明：选项 MEETING 错误，因为meeting 不强调久别重逢。；选项 REUNION 正确，因为reunion 描述久别相聚。

27. The WEATHER/ CLIMATE_ has been terrible lately.

- 答案：WEATHER
- 说明：选项 WEATHER 正确，因为weather 指短期天气状况。；选项 CLIMATE 错误，因为climate 指长期气候。

28. Many scientists think that the WEATHER/ CLIMATE is changing due to human actions.

- 答案：CLIMATE
- 说明：选项 WEATHER 错误，因为weather 的短期变化不表示趋势。；选项 CLIMATE 正确，因为climate 表示气候系统。

29. My friend set me up on a blind _____APPOINTMENT/DATE with one of her friends from work.

- 答案：DATE
- 说明：选项 APPOINTMENT 错误，因为appointment 指商务约定。；选项 DATE 正确，因为blind date 指相亲约会。

30. I can't stay long, I have an important ____ APPOINTMENT/DATE with a client.

- 答案：APPOINTMENT
- 说明：选项 APPOINTMENT 正确，因为与客户的正式约会用 appointment。；选项 DATE 错误，因为date 通常指浪漫约会。

31. I was LONELY/ ALONE at my house, so I played the music as loud as I wanted.

- 答案：ALONE
- 说明：选项 LONELY 错误，因为lonely 强调情绪。；选项 ALONE 正确，因为alone 表示独自一人。

32. Sometimes it's really LONELY/ ALONE living abroad by myself.

- 答案：LONELY
- 说明：选项 LONELY 正确，因为lonely 表示感到孤独。；选项 ALONE 错误，因为alone 只描述状态。

33. One of my kids is in college. The OTHER/ANOTHER one is still in high school.

- 答案：OTHER
- 说明：选项 OTHER 正确，因为the other 指两者中的另一个。；选项 ANOTHER 错误，因为another 通常用于三者及以上。

34. OTHER/ANOTHER thing I like about you is your honesty.

- 答案：ANOTHER
- 说明：选项 OTHER 错误，因为缺少限定词。；选项 ANOTHER 正确，因为another 表示‘另外一个’。

35. Remember to put a question mark and not a ____POINT/PERIOD/ DOT at the end of a question.

- 答案：PERIOD
- 说明：选项 POINT 错误，因为point 多用于几何或读数字。；选项 PERIOD 正确，因为美式英语中句号叫 period。；选项 DOT 错误，因为dot 常用在网址或小数点。

36. The diameter is 4.6 (read 'four _______ POINT/ PERIOD/ DOT six') centimeters.

- 答案：POINT
- 说明：选项 POINT 正确，因为读小数点时用 point。；选项 PERIOD 错误，因为period 不用于读数字。；选项 DOT 错误，因为dot 更常用于网址读法。

37. What type of web site domain should we get, a .com (read “POINT/ PERIOD/ DOT com”) site or a .org site?

- 答案：DOT
- 说明：选项 POINT 错误，因为常规读法用 dot。；选项 PERIOD 错误，因为period 不用于网址。；选项 DOT 正确，因为读网址中的点。

38. I _Missed / LOST the workshop because I was sick.

- 答案：MISSED
- 说明：选项 MISSED 正确，因为miss 表示错过活动。；选项 LOST 错误，因为lose 表示丢失或失败。

39. We _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _

- 答案：未知
- 说明：选项 —：题干和备选项缺失，无法补充说明。

40. There are a lot of birds sitting on the CEILING/ROOF of the building.

- 答案：ROOF
- 说明：选项 CEILING 错误，因为ceiling 指室内天花板。；选项 ROOF 正确，因为roof 指屋顶。

41. I have no idea how to attach this fan to the CEILING/ROOF

- 答案：CEILING
- 说明：选项 CEILING 正确，因为安装吊扇在天花板。；选项 ROOF 错误，因为屋顶不合适。

42. We should plant a GARDEN / YARD to save money on fruits and vegetables.

- 答案：GARDEN
- 说明：选项 GARDEN 正确，因为garden 可用于种植蔬果。；选项 YARD 错误，因为yard 指院子，没有种植含义。

43. We didn't buy the house because we wanted a bigger GARDEN / YARD for our dogs.

- 答案：YARD
- 说明：选项 GARDEN 错误，因为garden 更偏向栽培植物。；选项 YARD 正确，因为yard 给狗提供活动空间。

44. Why didn't he pass the ball? What a terrible _____. PLAY/ GAME

- 答案：PLAY
- 说明：选项 PLAY 正确，因为play 指单次配合或动作。；选项 GAME 错误，因为game 指整场比赛。

45. I don't want to miss the .PLAY/ GAME, so let's go home now and watch it.

- 答案：GAME
- 说明：选项 PLAY 错误，因为不指整场比赛。；选项 GAME 正确，因为game 表示整场赛事。

46. _BESIDE / BESIDES English and French, I also speak German.

- 答案：BESIDES
- 说明：选项 BESIDE 错误，因为beside 表示在旁边。；选项 BESIDES 正确，因为besides 表示除…之外还。

47. I keep a picture of my family _ BESIDE / BESIDES my computer monitor.

- 答案：BESIDE
- 说明：选项 BESIDE 正确，因为表示在旁边。；选项 BESIDES 错误，因为besides 表示此外。

48. Let's take a walk and SEE /LOOK AT / WATCH all the beautiful scenery.

- 答案：LOOK AT
- 说明：选项 SEE 错误，因为see 偏向被动感知。；选项 LOOK AT 正确，因为look at 表示主动欣赏。；选项 WATCH 错误，因为watch 多用在动态对象。

49. I can't SEE / LOOK AT / WATCH anything without my glasses.

- 答案：SEE
- 说明：选项 SEE 正确，因为表示看不见。；选项 LOOK AT 错误，因为需要有可见对象。；选项 WATCH 错误，因为watch 强调持续观看。

50. Please _ SEE /LOOK AT / WATCH_ the video and let me know if you have any questions.

- 答案：WATCH
- 说明：选项 SEE 错误，因为see 不强调专注。；选项 LOOK AT 错误，因为look at 多指稍微看一下。；选项 WATCH 正确，因为watch 表示专注地看视频。
