## Business English — Phrasal Verbs (get/give/go)

### Word Bank

**get :**

- get (something/someone) back 重新得到/挽回某人或客户
- get back at (someone) 报复某人
- get in 到达
- get over (something) 从某挫折中恢复或克服
- get together 聚会、相聚
- get up 起床
- get through (something) 克服（困难），完成（任务）
- get along (with someone) 相处融洽
- get by 应付，勉强过活
- get on (with something) 进展，进行，继续

**give :**

- give in 让步，屈服
- give in to (something/someone) 向（某事/某人）让步
- give up 放弃
- give (something) up 放弃（某事）
- give away 泄露（秘密）
- give back 归还
- give out 分发，发放
- give off 散发（气味、光等）

**go :**

- go after 追求、努力争取
- go over 复习；重温；（为了发现错误而）检查。
- go through 经历（通常指困难的事）；（按顺序）浏览/检查；搜寻。
- go back 返回，追溯
- go on 继续，发生
- go in 进入，加入
- go away 离开，消失
- go against 违背，反对

**take :**

- take off 脱掉，起飞
- take over 接管，接手
- take on 承担，接受（挑战、任务等）
- take part in 参加，参与
- take up 占据（时间、空间等），开始从事
- take up with 与......交往
- take in 吸收，理解
- take someone through 向某人详细讲解
- take no notice of 忽略，不理会
- take out of 从......中取出，带某人出去，让某人劳累
- take after 像（父母等），照顾
- take away 带走，夺走
- take back 收回，撤销

**put :**

- put off 推迟，延迟
- put up with 忍受，容忍

- call off 取消，叫停

- set off 引发，激起

**turn :**

- turn up 出现，露面
- turn down 拒绝，调低（音量等）
- turn on 打开，启动
- turn off 关闭，切断

### Fill in the Blanks

1. Let’s meet tomorrow so we can _____ _____ the details of the new contract.
   - 答案：go over（逐条检查）
   - 说明：我们明天见面，好把新合同的细节逐条过一遍。
   - 解析：`go over` 表示“仔细审查/复习”信息，强调逐项讨论。

2. They want lower prices on the next shipment, but we’re not going to _____ _____ to their demands.
   - 答案：give in（让步）
   - 说明：他们想要下一批货更低的价格，但我们不会向他们的要求让步。
   - 解析：`give in to` 表示屈服于压力或要求。

3. After the long work week, I didn’t _____ _____ until eleven o’clock in the morning.
   - 答案：get up（起床）
   - 说明：忙碌了一整个星期后，我直到上午十一点才起床。
   - 解析：`get up` 指“起床、起身”。

4. There are too many distractions at my house. I’m going to have to _____ _____ working from home.
   - 答案：give up（放弃）
   - 说明：家里太多干扰，我可能要放弃在家办公。
   - 解析：`give up` 表示“放弃、停止做某事”。

5. We lost one of our top clients to the competition. Now we’re doing everything to _____ him _____.
   - 答案：get him back（挽回他）
   - 说明：我们失去了一个重要客户，现在竭尽全力把他赢回来。
   - 解析：`get (someone) back` 意为“重新得到/挽回某人或客户”。

6. We’ve been really busy. I _____ _____ last night around ten o’clock.
   - 答案：got in（回到家/回来）
   - 说明：我们最近很忙，我昨晚大概十点才回到家。
   - 解析：`get in` 在口语里常指“到家”或“到达”。

7. Some of the guys from Accounting want to _____ _____ for drinks after work. Do you want to go?
   - 答案：get together（聚一聚）
   - 说明：财务部的同事想下班后聚在一起喝一杯。
   - 解析：`get together` 表示“聚会、相聚”。

8. I know it’s frustrating, but don’t _____ _____. I know you can do this.
   - 答案：give up（放弃）
   - 说明：我知道这让人沮丧，但别放弃，你能做到的。
   - 解析：`give up` 在此表示“停止努力、认输”。

9. Our company offers a bonus to the salesperson with the highest sales each month. I could really use the money, so I’m really going to _____ _____ it this month.
   - 答案：go after（积极争取）
   - 说明：公司每月给销售冠军发奖金，我需要这笔钱，所以这个月我要全力去争取。
   - 解析：`go after` 表示“追求、努力争取”某个目标。

10. If you want to get a promotion, it’s never a good idea to _____ _____ company policies.
    - 答案：go against（违背）
    - 说明：如果想升职，就不要违反公司的政策。
    - 解析：`go against` 表示“反对、违背”规则或指示。

11. Learning how to _____ _____ your failures is important in any job.
    - 答案：get over（走出、克服）
    - 说明：在任何工作中，学会走出失败、重新振作都很重要。
    - 解析：`get over` 指“从挫折中恢复或克服”。

12. He may try to _____ _____ at you for telling everyone about the two-hour lunch breaks he has been taking.
    - 答案：get back at（报复）
    - 说明：他可能因为你揭发他午休两小时而想报复你。
    - 解析：`get back at` 表示“报复某人”。

### Business Collocation Exercise

find one word that forms collocations with all the words in the row.

1. j--------: search for, look for, apply for, land, take, discard, hold do
   - 答案：job（工作/职位）；说明：动词搭配都与寻找、获得或辞掉工作有关；解析：常见搭配有 search for a job、land a job 等。

2. m-------------  arrange, organize, schedule, make, have, cancel, call off, chair
   - 答案：meeting（会议）；说明：这些动词都常与会议搭配；解析：chair a meeting 指“主持会议”。

3. a-------------t: disobey, negotiate, reach, break, go back on, violate
   - 答案：agreement（协议）；说明：可以与 reach/break an agreement 等搭配；解析：go back on an agreement 表示“违背协议”。

4. b---------: give, award, provide, pay, receive, earn
   - 答案：bonus（奖金）；说明：公司可以发放或员工获得奖金；解析：earn a bonus 表示“靠业绩挣得奖金”。

5. c--------------: bring about, make, do, cause
   - 答案：changes（变革/变化）；说明：可以 bring about changes、make changes 等；解析：cause changes 亦常见。

6. c----------------: reach, do, achieve, arrive at
   - 答案：consensus（共识）；说明：常说 reach/achieve/arrive at a consensus；解析：do a consensus 虽少见，但在练习里作同义延伸，指“达成一致”。

7. c-------------: file, make, have, receive, respond to, give
   - 答案：complaint（投诉）；说明：file/receive/respond to a complaint 等都是固定搭配；解析：在客服语境尤常见。

8. p----------------- make, suspend, perform, receive, stop, defer
   - 答案：payments（付款）；说明：可以 make/stop/defer payments；解析：suspend payments 指“暂停付款”。

9. ap-----------: have, make, keep, place, cancel, break
   - 答案：appointment（预约/约会）；说明：常说 make/keep/cancel an appointment；解析：break an appointment 表示“爽约”。

10. r--------------: propose, make, submit, turn down, reject, respond to, comply with
    - 答案：request（请求）；说明：可以 submit/respond to/comply with a request；解析：turn down a request 表示“拒绝请求”。

11. ri------: take, face, run, minimize, reduce, avoid, knock down, outweigh
    - 答案：risk（风险）；说明：take/run a risk、reduce/avoid risk 等都是常用搭配；解析：knock down risk 指“降低风险”。

12. a-------------: put, direct, give, pay, turn
    - 答案：attention（注意力）；说明：pay/turn/direct attention to...；解析：give attention to 意为“给予关注”。

13. d---------: agree to, arrange, close, draft, negotiate, reach, sign, seal
    - 答案：deal（交易）；说明：close/strike/sign a deal 等；解析：draft a deal 指拟定交易条款。

14. p---------------: pose, present, have, cause, set off, create, face, run into
    - 答案：problem（问题）；说明：pose/cause a problem、face/run into a problem；解析：set off a problem 表示“引发问题”。

15. so----------: look for, maneuver, work towards, agree on, come up with, propose, suggest
    - 答案：solution（解决方案）；说明：look for/work towards/agree on a solution；解析：come up with a solution 意为“想出办法”。

16. m------------t: break into, penetrate, pass into, corner, flood, enter
    - 答案：market（市场）；说明：break into/enter a market，corner/flood the market；解析：penetrate the market 指“打入市场”。

17. c-----------: cause, fuel, stir up, avoid, induce
    - 答案：conflict（冲突）；说明：cause/avoid/stir up conflict；解析：induce conflict 即“引发冲突”。

18. l---------: apply for, ask for, request, take out, gain, pay off, repay, secure
    - 答案：loan（贷款）；说明：apply for/take out/repay a loan；解析：secure a loan 表示成功获批。

19. in------------t: make, do, encourage, stimulate, protect, attract
    - 答案：investment（投资）；说明：make/attract/protect investment；解析：stimulate investment 指“刺激投资”。

20. o----------: make, withdraw, accept, decline, assert, refuse, turn down
    - 答案：offer（报价/提议）；说明：make/withdraw/accept an offer；解析：turn down an offer 表示“拒绝提议”。

21. c-------------: draw, jump to, arrive at, come to, do, reach
    - 答案：conclusion（结论）；说明：draw/jump to/arrive at a conclusion；解析：reach a conclusion 是常见商务表达。

22. p------------t: launch, develop, sell, market, cultivate, promote
    - 答案：product（产品）；说明：launch/develop/market a product；解析：cultivate a product 指“细化产品线”。

23. r-------------: dispense, take, shoulder, share, delegate
    - 答案：responsibility（责任）；说明：shoulder/share/delegate responsibility；解析：dispense responsibility 表示“分配责任”。

24. c-------------: have, haggle, draw up, write, negotiate, sign
    - 答案：contract（合同）；说明：draw up/write/sign a contract；解析：haggle over a contract 即“在合同上讨价还价”。

25. r--------: prepare, draw up, compile, submit, give, construct
    - 答案：report（报告）；说明：prepare/submit/give a report；解析：construct a report 指“搭建报告结构”。

26. c-------------r: deal with, drop, serve, attract, entice, lose
    - 答案：customer（顾客/客户）；说明：serve/attract/lose a customer；解析：deal with a customer 指“应对客户需求”。

27. s-------------: offer, dispense, provide, improve, expand
    - 答案：service（服务）；说明：offer/provide/improve service；解析：expand services 表示“扩充服务范围”。

28. l--------: enforce, uphold, comply with, obey, fulfill, violate, break
    - 答案：law（法律）；说明：enforce/uphold/comply with the law；解析：violate/break the law 为常见搭配。

29. p---------: implement, strike, adopt, have,
    - 答案：partnership（伙伴关系）；说明：strike/adopt/have a partnership；解析：implement a partnership 指把合作关系落到实处。

30. as-------: accumulate, acquire, get, sell, transfer
    - 答案：assets（资产）；说明：可以 accumulate/acquire/sell/transfer assets；解析：企业经常围绕资产进行此类操作。
