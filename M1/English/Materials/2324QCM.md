# Réponses commentées au QCM – Higher Education & Work

## 93. Higher Education

1. Harvard’s $35bn **endowment** supports teaching and research activities.
   a. expenditures b. **capital** c. budget

   * ✅ **Réponse : b) capital**
   * **Explication :** *Endowment* 指大学的捐赠基金或资本，用来支持长期研究与教学。`capital` 表示“资金、资本”最贴切；`expenditures` 是支出，`budget` 是预算，不合语义。

2. **Legacies** have a head start when it comes to admission to some colleges.
   a. fat cats b. confirmed athletes c. children of alumni

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) children of alumni**
   * **Explication :** *Legacies* 是指“校友子女”，在招生中通常享有优待。

3. As **tuition** rises, low-income families can’t afford a college education.
   a. allowances b. prices c. **fees**

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) fees**
   * **Explication :** *Tuition* 指“学费”，属于 fee 的一种。

4. The accreditation agencies rate universities and colleges on criteria such as the publishing records of their **faculty**.
   a. facilities b. students c. **professors**

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) professors**
   * **Explication :** *Faculty* 表示教学人员整体，即教授群体。

5. To **major** in Astrophysics you need an extra math course.
   a. be a first b. **specialize** c. better understand

   * ✅ **Réponse : b) specialize**
   * **Explication :** *Major in* = specialize in（主修、专攻）。

6. The Virginia Military Institute was the last state institution of higher learning to become **coeducational**.
   a. to accept blacks b. **to accept females** c. to accept civilians

   * ✅ **Réponse : b) to accept females**
   * **Explication :** *Coeducational* 指“男女同校的”。

7. I just can’t cope. There are too many **assignments** in Prof. Jones’ class.
   a. tests b. **homework** c. duties

   * ✅ **Réponse : b) homework**
   * **Explication :** *Assignment* 是布置的作业或任务。

8. “Did you know that Mary was a **sophomore**?” – “You mean she’s in …?”
   a. first year b. **second year** c. fourth year

   * ✅ **Réponse : b) second year**
   * **Explication :** *Sophomore* 指大二学生（1st freshman, 2nd sophomore, 3rd junior, 4th senior）。

9. As the student having obtained the best grades, she was asked to deliver the keynote speech at the commencement ceremony.
   a. salutatorian b. **valedictorian** c. major

   * ✅ **Réponse : b) valedictorian**
   * **Explication :** *Valedictorian* 是毕业典礼上代表发言的优秀学生。

10. Johns Hopkins’ **former students** have been asked to contribute money towards the building of a new medical research lab.
    a. alma mater b. elders c. **alumni**

    * ✅ **Réponse : c) alumni**
    * **Explication :** *Alumni* 指校友、毕业生。

11. **Enrollment** of foreign science students went down after 9/11.
    a. withdrawal b. **registration** c. scholarships

    * ✅ **Réponse : b) registration**
    * **Explication :** *Enrollment* 指注册或入学人数。

12. **Phi Beta Kappa**’s mission is to foster and recognize excellence.
    a. **honor society** b. Greek association c. faculty club

    * ✅ **Réponse : a) honor society**
    * **Explication :** Phi Beta Kappa 是美国最古老的学术荣誉协会。

13. As enrollment continues to increase, university officials have been forced to house some freshmen in **upperclassmen** residence halls.
    a. junior b. graduate c. **senior**

    * ✅ **Réponse : c) senior**
    * **Explication :** *Upperclassmen* 指高年级学生（junior 或 senior）。

## 94. Work

1. In most western nations the … population is shrinking.
   a. work b. worker c. **working**

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) working**
   * **Explication :** “working population” 指劳动人口。

2. The reforms implemented in France to lower employment by reducing the … have not given the expected results.
   a. **work week** b. working week c. worked week

   * ✅ **Réponse : a) work week**
   * **Explication :** “work week” = 工作周，正确搭配。

3. Some industries hire foreign national workers because of shortages of qualified candidates in the local labor …
   a. reserve b. tank c. **pool**

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) pool**
   * **Explication :** “labor pool” 表示劳动力储备。

4. About 20 million Americans perform …, which means they get less sleep over 24 hours than day workers.
   a. flex-time b. **shift work** c. temping

   * ✅ **Réponse : b) shift work**
   * **Explication :** *Shift work* = 轮班制工作。

5. Because of the existence of what is called a glass …, high-caliber female executives are prevented from holding top managerial positions.
   a. wall b. floor c. **ceiling**

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) ceiling**
   * **Explication :** “glass ceiling” 指女性升迁的无形障碍。

6. Work-… was first introduced in the Netherlands in the 1980s to encourage a wider range of people to participate in the workforce.
   a. pooling b. dividing c. **sharing**

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) sharing**
   * **Explication :** *Work-sharing* 指“工作分配制”，旨在减少失业。

7. California is making money available to help re-… construction workers who can’t find jobs because of the housing crisis.
   a. **train** b. cycle c. form

   * ✅ **Réponse : a) train**
   * **Explication :** *Retrain* 表示再培训。

8. A hundred years ago, the first Model T rolled off Ford Motors …
   a. shop floor b. mass production c. **assembly line**

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) assembly line**
   * **Explication :** *Assembly line* 是流水线。

9. After the snowstorm, road crews worked … as shifts were extended in order to keep round-the-clock plowing going.
   a. more hours b. extra periods c. **overtime**

   * ✅ **Réponse : c) overtime**
   * **Explication :** *Overtime* 意为“加班”。

10. Most of our employees choose … so they can adjust their schedule according to personal choice and convenience.
    a. flex hours b. **flexitime** c. variable time

    * ✅ **Réponse : b) flexitime**
    * **Explication :** *Flexitime* 是弹性工作时间制度。

11. Some hard-up students … as taxi drivers or bouncers to make both ends meet.
    a. **moonlight** b. pilfer c. rehearse

    * ✅ **Réponse : a) moonlight**
    * **Explication :** *To moonlight* 表示“兼职”或“做第二份工作”。

12. Rather than hire and risk having to fire in another downturn, companies added hours for those on the … and relied on temporary workers.
    a. pay scale b. **payroll** c. pay list

    * ✅ **Réponse : b) payroll**
    * **Explication :** *On the payroll* 表示“在公司雇员名单上”。
