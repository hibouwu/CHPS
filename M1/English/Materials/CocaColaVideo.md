# English test 英语测试

Dr John Stith Pemberton: addicted to morphine after an accident. He created this drink initially for himself. From the beginning, Coca-Cola was a scam: it was labeled as a medicinal beverage when in reality it was just a copy of an already existing French drink called *vin Mariani* with cocaine added to it.
约翰·斯蒂斯·彭巴顿医生在一次事故后对吗啡上瘾。他最初为了自己而调制这种饮料。从一开始，可口可乐就是个骗局：它被贴上药用饮料的标签，但实际上只是照搬已经存在的法国饮品“马里阿尼葡萄酒”，并加入了可卡因。

Towards the end of his life, John was in debt; he sold Coca-Cola for only **$2300** to **Isaac Handler** (marketer and lawyer). Isaac changed the label from a medicinal beverage to a drink:
晚年时约翰债台高筑；他仅以**2300美元**把可口可乐卖给了市场营销律师**艾萨克·汉德勒**。艾萨克把它从药用饮料改成普通饮品，并说：

> “You don’t sell a product, you sell the feeling it creates.”
> “你卖的不是产品，你卖的是它带来的感觉。”

In the late 1890s, at **5 cents a glass**, the drink had to be mixed on the counter and people had to go to the drugstore to consume it. Two lawyers came up to Isaac with the idea of bottling the drink. Isaac didn’t believe in this idea and to get rid of them sold the rights to it for only **$1**.
19世纪90年代末，一杯饮料只要**5美分**，必须在柜台现场调制，人们得去药店才能喝。两位律师向艾萨克提出把饮料装瓶的想法。艾萨克不看好，为了打发他们仅以**1美元**卖出了装瓶权。

Then there is **Robert Woodruff**, who transformed Coca-Cola into a worldwide brand during WW2. He sold the bottles for **5 cents/drink for every soldier**. Coca-Cola became a symbol of patriotism and part of the war effort. You were contributing just as much if you were making Coca-Cola bottles as if you were fighting at the front.
之后是**罗伯特·伍德拉夫**，他在二战期间把可口可乐打造为全球品牌。他让每位士兵都能以**每瓶5美分**的价格买到。可口可乐成为爱国主义的象征，也是战争努力的一部分。制造可口可乐瓶子被视为与上前线作战同样重要的贡献。

Coca-Cola became what it is today because of their shady strategies and genius marketing ideas, very often ahead of their time.
可口可乐之所以发展到今天，是因为那些灰色策略与超前的营销点子。

## Ideas 想法

1. **1886**: Atlanta banned alcohol, so John replaced the wine with syrup (water + sugar), which is also addictive.
   **1886**：亚特兰大禁酒，因此约翰用糖浆（水加糖）取代了葡萄酒，这种东西同样令人上瘾。
2. John bought lists of clients from drugstores and gave them free samples to get them hooked.
   约翰从药店购买客户名单，派发免费试饮让人们上瘾。
3. Creating a contest to get people to design the Coca-Cola bottle so that it’s distinctive and recognizable from a mile away.
   发起比赛请人设计可口可乐的瓶形，使其即使相隔一英里也能认出。
4. Lobbying the government to bend the rationing rules that prevented soldiers from having too much sugar. The spread of Coca-Cola worldwide was then financed by the government. Production was cheaper in countries with cheap labor (Latin America…).
   游说政府放宽限制士兵摄糖的配给制度。此后政府为可口可乐在全球扩张买单，而在劳动力廉价的国家（如拉美）生产成本更低。
5. Playing both sides during the war: pretending to be a symbol of American patriotism but selling **Fanta** (which they owned) in Nazi Germany where Coca-Cola was restricted.
   战时两面下注：一方面装作美国爱国主义的象征，另一方面在禁止可口可乐的纳粹德国卖他们旗下的**芬达**。
6. **Santa Claus**: associating a drink with a worldwide celebration.
   **圣诞老人**：把饮料与全球性的节日庆典绑定在一起。
7. Using the red color as advertising (they even painted barns in Atlanta in red for that purpose — which became a tradition).
   把红色作为广告色调（他们甚至在亚特兰大把谷仓都刷成红色，后来成了传统）。
8. Creating a whole department that studied traffic and the movement of people in stores (red lights, crossroads) to optimize advertising.
   建立专门部门研究交通和店内人流（红绿灯、十字路口），以优化广告投放。
9. First company to do sponsorships/endorsements with celebrities and athletes.
   第一家与名人和运动员合作做赞助与代言的公司。
10. **Cradle-to-grave strategy**: getting kids hooked on sugar as early as possible. Deals with schools to sell Coca-Cola in vending machines. They bypass rules through loopholes and advertise to kids by selling “collectible items” that look like toys.
    **从摇篮到坟墓策略**：尽早让孩子迷上糖分，与学校合作在售货机里卖可口可乐。通过钻漏洞规避限制，并以类似玩具的“收藏品”向孩子做广告。
11. Vertical integration: they own everything — producers, trucks, etc.
    垂直整合：他们拥有从生产到运输等每个环节。
12. In the 90s, more data showing the harm of Coca-Cola was released. It was a major setback, so they paid labs to say the opposite.
    90年代曝光了更多可口可乐危害的数据，这是一次重大打击，于是他们付钱让实验室发表相反结论。
13. With their water **Dasani** in the UK, they were caught selling tap water.
    在英国，他们的**达沙尼**瓶装水被揭露只是自来水。
14. They diversify drinks to appeal to everyone (Minute Maid, Fanta, Sprite, Powerade, Root Beer) and make it seem like some drinks are healthy, though they are also full of sugar (e.g., Powerade).
    通过多元化的饮料（美汁源、芬达、雪碧、力量Ade、沙士等）来吸引所有人，并让一些饮品看似健康，尽管同样充满糖分（如力量Ade）。
15. Benefits from shady deals with politicians: in Mexico, the head of Coca-Cola Latin America became president. He gave **27 water-pumping concessions** to Coca-Cola, increasing sales by 50% and causing a water drought.
    借助与政客的隐秘交易：在墨西哥，可口可乐拉丁美洲的负责人当上了总统。他向公司授予**27项取水特许权**，销量因此增长50%，却引发水资源枯竭。
16. The drink is cheaper than water there, so babies drink it and get immediately addicted.
    当地可乐比水还便宜，婴儿都在喝，很快就上瘾。
17. Coca-Cola is embedded in Mayan culture, where some believe it has religious significance.
    可口可乐被嵌入玛雅文化，有些人认为它具有宗教意义。
18. In **1968**, Coca-Cola was the sponsor of Mexico in the Olympics.
    **1968年**，可口可乐是墨西哥奥运代表团的赞助商。
19. When they changed the taste (blind tests showed it was better), sales dropped — showing people were attached to the image, not the taste. They brought back **Classic Coke**, causing a huge boost. Some say this “blunder” was planned for publicity.
    当他们调整口味时（盲测显示更好喝），销量反而下滑——说明人们看中的是形象而非味道。他们重新推出**经典可乐**，销量飙升。有些人说这一“失误”是为博宣传而设计的。
20. To circumvent sugar-intake limits, they add sweeteners instead of sugar, which can also cause health issues.
    为规避糖摄入限制，他们用代糖替代蔗糖，但代糖同样可能带来健康问题。

## Vocabulary

## **1. addicted to**

* 含义：对……上瘾
* 注意：后面接名词或动名词。
* 示例：*addicted to morphine*

## **2. initially**

* 含义：最初，起初
* 常用于叙述历史或过程。

## **3. scam**

* 含义：骗局
* 语气强烈，多用在非正式或批判语境。

## **4. labeled as**

* 含义：被标记/被宣传为……
* 注意：被动语态常用。

## **5. in reality**

* 含义：实际上
* 用于纠正误解或强调事实。

## **6. towards the end of his life**

* 含义：在他生命的晚期
* 高级短语，可用于写作。

## **7. in debt**

* 含义：负债中
* 固定搭配。

## **8. rights**

* 含义：权利（这里指产品销售权）
* 在商业语境中很常见：*selling the rights to…*

## **9. worldwide brand**

* 含义：全球品牌
* 考试中可替换为 *global brand*。

## **10. patriotism**

* 含义：爱国主义
* 较正式的词。

## **11. ahead of their time**

* 含义：超前于时代
* 英语口语和写作都很常见的表达。

## **12. ban**

* 含义：禁止
* 注意：*ban sb from doing sth* 或 *ban + noun*。

## **13. addictive**

* 含义：令人上瘾的
* 形容词，和 *addicted* 不同。

## **14. free samples**

* 含义：免费样品
* 营销相关常用词。

## **15. lobbying / lobby**

* 含义：游说
* 政治与商业领域常考词汇。

## **16. rationing**

* 含义：配给，定量供应
* 常用于战争或资源短缺语境。

## **17. pretend to**

* 含义：假装
* *pretend to do sth*

## **18. shortage / drought**

* 含义：短缺 / 干旱
* 文中：water drought（缺水）

## **19. embedded in**

* 含义：深深嵌入，成为……的一部分
* 比较高级的表达。

## **20. blind tasting**

* 含义：盲测
* 注意：不是“盲人品尝”，而是“事先不知道品牌的品尝”。

## **21. publicity**

* 含义：宣传、曝光度
* 常和 marketing、branding 一起出现。

## **22. circumvent**

* 含义：规避
* 语气正式；可替换 avoid / get around。
